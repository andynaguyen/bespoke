## Apple Speech is a model

On macOS 26, the Models page lists Apple Speech. It runs on this Mac with Apple's speech model and needs no download from Bespoke, so you can dictate as soon as you finish onboarding.

## Annotate uses your model and dictionary

Annotate transcribes a capture with the model picked on the Models page and fixes names with your dictionary. With no model picked, or if the picked one fails, Apple Speech runs.

## Dictionary has its own page

The dictionary applies to Dictate and Annotate, so it moved from a Dictate tab to its own page in the sidebar. Its Try it panel shows what a dictation would paste while the page is open, without pasting it: each fix the dictionary made, the entries that fired, and a way to add a misheard word as a word or a replacement.

## Styles on a board

Dictate > Style is now a board with a column per style. Drag an app onto a style, or click it. The Default chip sets the style for apps without a rule.
