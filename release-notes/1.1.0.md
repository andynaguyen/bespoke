## A guided first run

Keycap Coach walks new users through setup: the microphone and Accessibility permissions, a practice dictation, Screen Recording, and a practice capture with Annotate. Bespoke relaunches after you grant Screen Recording, and the tour picks up where it left off. Practice takes stay on screen and never paste.

## A quieter capsule

Dictate and Annotate now share one smaller recording capsule with a still dot and a gray meter. Its buttons show for the first 3.5 seconds and when you hover, then fold away. Esc and your shortcuts work either way. After a paste, Dictate shows a check. If the paste fails, the capsule says "Couldn't paste" instead of claiming success.

## Annotate

- Shortcut Behavior now applies to Annotate. Hold records while you hold the shortcut, Auto picks hold or toggle from how long you press, and Toggle stays the default.
- Set your own Cancel Shortcut. Esc used to be the only way to cancel.
- Annotate recordings lower other audio and play the start and stop sounds, as Dictate does.

## Sound

A slider replaces the Mute While Recording toggle. It lowers other audio to a share of its volume, from 0% for silence to 100% for no change. If you had mute on, you start at 0%. If you change the volume during a recording, Bespoke leaves it alone when the recording ends.

## Fixes

- The whole app now shows in your language, including the menu bar, the Annotate picker and the permission panel. 244 strings had no translation before.
- Model names with a dot, like parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3, now show their translated descriptions.
