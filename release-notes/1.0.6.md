## A priority list of microphones

Settings > Sound now keeps an ordered list of microphones instead of a single pick. Dictate and Annotate record from the first one that's connected. Unplug your desk mic and the next recording uses the laptop's. Plug it back in and the one after that uses the desk mic again, with no re-picking.

A mic you unplug stays in the list, marked Not connected, until you remove it. Drag the rows to reorder them. The mic you had picked before becomes the first entry.
