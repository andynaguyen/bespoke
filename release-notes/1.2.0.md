## A clearer History

Selecting a dictation in History now shows the app it went to, with that app's icon, above the time. The text that pasted comes first. When post-processing changed it, a Post-processed tag marks it and the original transcript sits in its own card with a Copy button. The recording plays from a waveform you can click or drag to seek. Delete, Show in Finder, Re-transcribe and Copy sit in the top row.

- Copy copies the text that pasted. It used to copy the original transcript.
- Show in Finder selects that dictation's recording.
- Playback stops when you pick another dictation or leave History.
- Dictations from before this version show their app too, read from your usage stats.

## Starring is gone

You can no longer star a dictation. Dictations you starred before now follow your History limit like the rest, so the next dictation removes any outside that limit, along with their recordings.

## Fixes

- Clicking Settings while another app was in front could freeze Bespoke until you force quit it.
- A stuck shortcut could freeze the window while you recorded a new shortcut. Bespoke now waits at most 2 seconds.
- The Accessibility banner's button no longer gets cut off in longer languages.
- Arabic shows times in 24-hour form, since its 12-hour form printed no AM or PM.
