## Every setting in one dialog

Settings used to live in four places: a Settings page, a Settings tab on Dictate, another on Annotate, and a panel inside Dictate > History. Now they share one Settings dialog. Its sidebar lists General, Appearance and Sound, then Dictate's and Annotate's pages, then System and Experimental. Open it with the gear at the foot of the sidebar, Cmd+, or the tray menu.

## Annotate records from your Sound microphone

Annotate records from the microphone picked on the Sound page, the same one Dictate uses. Before, the two could each use a different mic.

## Triage from the sidebar

Triage an Issue moved from Settings > System to the bug button next to the Settings gear. It walks through three steps: show the problem with an Annotate capture (optional), pick an agent, and start. A capture you finish while the dialog is open, or in the 10 minutes before you open it, goes to the agent, which reads it before anything else.
